Blurly — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Chrome extension Blurly — Privacy Screen for WhatsApp Web. Last updated 21 August 2026.

The short version: Blurly collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. It has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your messages, contacts and photos never leave your browser.

What Blurly does

Blurly applies a CSS blur over the WhatsApp Web chat list and open conversation so people near your screen can't read them. Hovering a blurred area reveals it while your cursor is there, and keyboard shortcuts toggle blur or hide everything instantly. It runs only on web.whatsapp.com.

Data we collect

None. Blurly does not collect, record, transmit, sell or share any data — including personally identifiable information, message content, contact names or photos, browsing history, location, or usage analytics. There is no crash or telemetry reporting.

Data stored on your device

Blurly saves a few true/false settings using Chrome's storage.local API — whether blur is on, and whether presenting mode is on. These stay in your own browser, are never transmitted, and are deleted when you uninstall the extension. No message content or contact information is ever stored.

Network activity

Blurly makes zero network requests. All code and styling ship inside the extension package; nothing is fetched, downloaded or executed from a remote source.

Blurly Pro

Blurly Pro is a separate paid build, not an in-app unlock. Payments are processed by Razorpay, who collect the email address and phone number you enter at their checkout under their own privacy policy. That email is used solely to send you the Pro build and to answer support about your purchase. It is not used for marketing and is not shared with anyone else. The free extension itself never sees or transmits this information — clicking "Get Pro" simply opens the Razorpay page in a new tab.

Children

Blurly is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.
